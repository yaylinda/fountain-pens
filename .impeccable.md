## Design Context

### Users

Linda uses this personal collection to find her fountain pens and inks, track refills, and correct inventory and journal entries. Frequent actions must be easy to find and complete, including on a phone.

### Brand Personality

Tactile, calm, personal. A useful writing desk with the pleasure of a well-kept stationery collection.

### Aesthetic Direction

Warm paper, dark aubergine ink, restrained brass details, and meaningful ink swatches. Bodoni Moda headings paired with Figtree controls and body text. Light surfaces suit comparing ink colors. This direction is the stated default offered during discovery; the user requested a simple, visually pleasing personal dashboard without specifying a palette.

Typography selection: rejected reflex choices Cormorant, Newsreader, and DM Sans; consulted the Google Fonts catalog for a printed stationery-label quality in Bodoni Moda and readable, compact Figtree.

### Design Principles

1. Put a pen, its latest ink, and its history together.
2. Use color to represent ink, never arbitrary brands or nib sizes.
3. Prefer dedicated editing workspaces with visible labels and searchable choices.
4. Preserve the three source JSON collections and historical relationships.
5. Keep deployment and synchronization outside the primary collection workflow.
